Help & FAQ

Find answers to common questions about FakeOut.

FakeOut is a web-based tool that helps you check whether a website is safe, suspicious, or potentially a phishing site before you share any personal information.

Simply paste a website URL into the analysis bar on our homepage. FakeOut will scan the site for known threats, suspicious patterns, SSL issues, and domain reputation to give you a clear risk score.

Safe (0–30): The website appears legitimate with no known threats.
Suspicious (31–70): Some warning signs detected — proceed with caution.
Dangerous (71–100): High risk of phishing or malware. Avoid sharing any data.
